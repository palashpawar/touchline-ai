# Quality signals

Generated by `scripts/gen_signal_doc.py` from `backend/touchline/quality.py`. Every signal is a measured
quantity with a unit and a threshold chosen by inspecting the development clip and the stress tests. **None of these is a
calibrated probability**; they are heuristic indicators. `n/a` means not measurable for that segment.

| Key | Signal | Unit | Breach when | Threshold | Definition | Limitations |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `calibration_valid` | Calibration valid | bool | below | 1 | Four-point calibration passed degeneracy, ordering, horizon and scale checks. | A valid homography can still be inaccurate far from the calibrated rectangle. |
| `camera_drift_px` | Camera drift vs calibration frame | px | above | 12.0 | Max displacement of the calibration rectangle's corners between the reference frame and any sampled frame, from the validated frame-to-reference homography. | Only measurable when the motion model validates; zoom changes appear as drift too. |
| `line_alignment` | Pitch-line alignment | fraction | below | 0.6 | Median over sampled frames of the share of detected pitch-line pixels that fall within 4 px of a reference-frame line pixel after mapping with the geometry used for projection. | White kit, shadows and line wear add noise; needs >=200 visible line pixels per frame. |
| `motion_inlier_ratio` | Camera-motion inlier ratio | fraction | below | 0.45 | Median MAGSAC inlier ratio of the frame-to-reference homography over sampled frames. | High ratios on a near-static camera are uninformative; crowd motion lowers it. |
| `motion_reproj_px` | Camera-motion reprojection error | px | above | 2.5 | Median (over frames) of the median inlier reprojection error of the motion homography. | Measured at half resolution and rescaled. |
| `motion_validated_fraction` | Frames with validated motion | fraction | below | 0.8 | Share of sampled frames whose motion estimate (to the calibration frame, or chained) met inlier-count, ratio, error and sanity limits. Low = the calibration can no longer be verified for this segment. | A static camera over a texture-poor scene can also fail to validate; the agent then asks rather than assumes. |
| `out_of_pitch_fraction` | Projected locations outside pitch | fraction | above | 0.12 | Share of detections whose foot is well inside the grass region (>=20 px from its edge) but whose projected ground point falls >3 m outside the pitch. | Assistant referees stand outside the touchline legitimately; grass-mask leakage can still admit edge spectators. Rises when calibration or camera geometry is wrong. |
| `implausible_jump_rate` | Implausible projected jumps | fraction | above | 0.08 | Share of consecutive same-track observations implying a ground speed >12 m/s. | Far-field projection noise can produce false jumps; needs valid calibration. |
| `short_track_ratio` | Short (fragmented) tracks | fraction | above | 0.3 | Share of tracks spanning < 1 s of the segment. | Players entering/leaving the view legitimately create short tracks. |
| `fragmentation_index` | Track fragmentation index | tracks per visible player | above | 1.6 | Number of tracks divided by the median number of kept detections per sampled frame. ~1.0 means one track per visible player. | Inflated by view changes; deflated if the detector misses players consistently. |
| `assoc_residual_h` | Association residual | box heights | above | 0.6 | Median distance between a track's Kalman prediction and its matched detection, in box heights. | Low values do not prove correct identity: two players can swap with low residual. |
| `detection_gap_fraction` | Detection gaps | fraction | above | 0.15 | Share of sampled frames with fewer than half the segment's median kept detections. | Occlusion by spectators in front of the camera is a natural cause the agent cannot fix. |
| `extrapolated_fraction` | Points far from calibration | fraction | above | 0.5 | Share of projected player points more than 30 m from the calibrated rectangle (informational). | Homography error grows with distance from the calibrated area. |

## Triggers

Breaches of `camera_drift_px`, `line_alignment`, `motion_validated_fraction`, `out_of_pitch_fraction`, `implausible_jump_rate`, `short_track_ratio`, `fragmentation_index`, `assoc_residual_h`, `detection_gap_fraction` drive `PLAN_RECOVERY`. Camera signals (`camera_drift_px`, `line_alignment`, `motion_validated_fraction`) lead to the camera-motion update (if the motion model validates) or to review; tracking signals (`short_track_ratio`, `fragmentation_index`, `assoc_residual_h`, `implausible_jump_rate`, `detection_gap_fraction`) lead to denser sampling. `camera_drift_px` stops counting once a segment runs motion-compensated.

## Motion-model validation

Validated when ≥ 40 MAGSAC inliers, inlier ratio ≥ 0.45, median inlier reprojection error ≤ 2.5 px (estimated at 960 px width, rescaled), and 0.5 < det(A) < 2 with small perspective terms.

## Acceptance rule (compare_runs)

On identical evaluation frames: at least one target signal improves ≥ 10% (relative); no target gets ≥ 10% worse while ending beyond its threshold; guards: line alignment may not drop > 0.05, off-pitch share may not rise > 0.05, kept detections per frame may not fall > 20%. Camera-motion updates are judged on `line_alignment` and `out_of_pitch_fraction`; densify on the breached tracking signals.

Retry budget 2 per segment; stride schedule [6, 3, 2, 1].
